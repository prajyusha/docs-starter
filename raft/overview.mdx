## Threading model in brief

Most state is owned by the **main goroutine** (`run` → `runFollower`/`runCandidate`/`runLeader` in `raft.go`). Other goroutines reach it through channels: `configurationChangeCh`, `configurationsCh`, `applyCh`, `verifyCh`, `userRestoreCh`. The leader runs one `replicate` goroutine plus one `heartbeat` goroutine per peer (`replication.go`), and a separate `runSnapshots` goroutine takes snapshots (`snapshot.go`). Functions marked "must only be called from the main thread" in the source must stay that way if you change them.
